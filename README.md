# AEGIS

### Enterprise AI Knowledge & Business Automation Copilot

> **An intelligent enterprise AI system designed to understand organizational knowledge, reason over business data, and automate real-world workflows.**

---

## 🚧 Project Status

**Early Development — Project Foundation**

AEGIS is currently in the **brainstorming and architecture-design phase**.

The initial application entry point has been established in `app.py`. The system architecture, HLD, LLD, agent design, data flow, and technology decisions will be developed collaboratively before implementation begins.

---

## 🧠 What is AEGIS?

Modern organizations have information scattered across documents, databases, cloud platforms, communication systems, and business applications.

Finding information is one problem.

**Turning that information into action is another.**

AEGIS aims to bridge that gap.

The long-term vision is to build an **Enterprise AI Copilot** capable of understanding natural-language requests, retrieving organizational knowledge, querying structured business data, reasoning over information, coordinating specialized AI agents, and executing authorized business workflows through connected tools.
